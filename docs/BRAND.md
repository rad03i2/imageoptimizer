# ImageOptimizer Visual Identity

The identity is built around **bringing an image inward without losing its purpose**: an image frame, inward arrows, layered image cards, and transform pixels.

## Personality

Creative, technical, premium, and visually tactile. It avoids generic AI, cyber-security, cloud, and document-tool aesthetics.

## Palette

| Role | Name | Hex |

## Logo

The square mark combines a framed image, landscape motif, four inward compression arrows, and detached transform pixels. It avoids a plain lettermark and remains readable as an image utility at small size.

Primary asset: `assets/project-logo.svg`.

## Cover

The hero uses layered image cards, a central framed image, compression motion, and the real supported format labels JPEG, PNG, and WebP. It intentionally includes no fake screenshot or fictional compression percentage.

Primary asset: `assets/project-cover.svg`.

## Typography

Use `Inter, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif`; Arabic fallback is `Tahoma, Arial, sans-serif`.

## Rules

- Do not stretch the logo.
- Keep clear space of roughly one-sixth of its width.
- Use coral/amber for transformation and violet for format/creative accents.
- Do not advertise a guaranteed savings percentage.
- Prefer dark or neutral backgrounds.

## Distinction

ImageOptimizer is intentionally warm, visual, and transformation-focused. It should not resemble OCR/document tooling, observability dashboards, security products, or generic AI brands.
